# HAW-Travel-Booking-Analysis
📊 Haw Travels – Data Analyst Internship
📌 Internship Overview

This repository contains the work completed during my Data Analyst Internship at Haw Travels. The primary focus of the internship was to analyze travel booking data, identify customer and destination trends, evaluate revenue performance, and develop an interactive Power BI dashboard for business insights.

The project demonstrates the use of Microsoft Excel, Power BI, Pivot Tables, Data Analysis, and DAX to transform raw travel booking data into meaningful business insights.

The main objectives of this internship project were:

🔍 Business Insights

The analysis produced several important insights:

Dubai generated the highest revenue among all destinations.
Bali was the second-highest revenue-generating destination.
The Premium customer segment generated the highest revenue despite having fewer customers than the Budget segment.
The Budget segment had the highest number of customers.
Dubai had the highest cancellation rate, indicating a need to investigate the reasons behind cancellations.
Goa had the lowest cancellation rate, suggesting comparatively stable booking demand.
The customer base was relatively balanced in terms of gender.
Customers aged 36–40 represented the largest age group in the dataset.
💡 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

Investigate the reasons for the high cancellation rate for Dubai packages.
Introduce attractive offers and loyalty programs for premium customers.
Develop targeted marketing campaigns for different customer segments.
Promote destinations with low cancellation rates.
Analyze seasonal booking patterns to optimize promotional campaigns.
Offer personalized packages based on customer age and spending behavior.
Monitor destination-level revenue and cancellation KPIs regularly.
